Agent safety testbed

Work a simulated incident with a browser agent. The agent can investigate and propose, but production actions only appear after the human approval gate opens.

What to try

Pick a scenario, ask an agent to investigate, challenge its hypothesis in the Human Console, then approve or reject its request.

Why WebMCP

Dangerous tools are phase-scoped: before approval, the agent cannot see the rollback tool instead of merely being told no.
